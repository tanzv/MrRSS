# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Privacy-conscious RSS readers, knowledge workers, researchers, and technically inclined readers who follow many sources and want a fast desktop workflow. They use the app for repeated daily reading, triage, saving, filtering, and revisiting articles across feeds and languages.

## Product Purpose

MrRSS is a local-first, cross-platform RSS reader that helps people collect, scan, understand, and organize articles without surrendering their reading data to an analytics platform. The product combines dependable feed management with optional local/cloud AI features, translation, automation, and integrations. Success means a reader can move from “new items arrived” to “I know what matters and can find it again” with minimal friction, while retaining control over privacy, layout, and visual theme.

## Brand Personality

Calm, capable, and configurable. The interface should feel trustworthy during long reading sessions, make advanced features discoverable without forcing them into the primary path, and let users make the visual style their own through a coherent set of built-in themes and extensible theme tokens.

## Anti-references

- Cloud-reader experiences that monetize attention through tracking, noisy recommendations, or engagement badges.
- One-size-fits-all dashboards that expose every advanced control at once and make a daily reading task feel like administration.
- Theme systems that only swap a dark/light flag while leaving contrast, component states, and hierarchy inconsistent.
- Decorative “AI-generated” visual patterns (generic card grids, gratuitous gradients/glows, or ornamental motion) that compete with article content.

## Design Principles

1. **Reading comes first.** Feed triage, article comprehension, and return-to-context should remain the clearest path on every screen.
2. **Power without intimidation.** Keep advanced automation, AI, and integrations available through progressive disclosure rather than making them the default surface.
3. **User-owned atmosphere.** Built-in themes and customization should be coherent, complete, and safe—not a collection of isolated color overrides.
4. **Earn trust through clarity.** Show refresh, translation, sync, caching, and destructive-action states plainly; avoid hidden work and ambiguous feedback.
5. **Local-first by default.** Privacy choices and data boundaries should be understandable at the moment they matter, without marketing-style persuasion.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Use WCAG 2.1 AA as the review baseline. Preserve keyboard access and visible focus, do not communicate state by color alone, support readable text scaling and both English and Simplified Chinese, and honor `prefers-reduced-motion`. No additional user-specific accessibility requirements have been supplied yet.
